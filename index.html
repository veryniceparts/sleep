About

Sleep Monitor
Version dev

Records the whole night and saves a clip of every loud sound. Afterwards it recognises the sounds (snoring, coughing, talking, …) on your phone with Google's YAMNet model via MediaPipe. Nothing is uploaded: recordings stay on this device.

⚠️ Not a medical device and can't diagnose sleep apnea. A sleep study is the only reliable diagnosis.
